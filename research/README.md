# Research sessions

One folder per session: `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/` containing `BRIEF.md` (written **before** searching), `SOURCES.md` (every source consulted, with claims and lineage), and `FINDINGS.md` (answers, grades, retrospective). Templates live in [../method/templates/](../method/templates/); rules in [../method/research-protocol.md](../method/research-protocol.md).

These folders are the audit trail for everything the primer claims. Keep extracts short and attributed — links, not wholesale copies; bulk raw material stays out of git.
