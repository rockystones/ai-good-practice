# P-003 — Trace the number to its table

**One-liner.** Never repeat a statistic without having seen the primary table or figure it came from.

**When.** Any number that reached you through a summary layer: journalism, paper abstracts, review articles, meeting decks, social posts — and AI-generated summaries, which are a summary layer with extra confidence. Especially before you republish, decide, or argue with it.

**Do.**

- Find the table/figure the number came out of. Check four things: the **denominator** (percentage *of what*?), the **units of analysis** (people? claims? coded fragments?), the **population/model/condition** it was measured on, and the **vintage** (is a newer measurement out?).
- If the primary is unreachable, keep the qualitative claim and drop the number — "studies find users over-trust confident output" survives scrutiny; a detached "93%" doesn't.
- After any compression step you perform yourself — summarizing, drafting, slide-making — re-diff your numbers against the source. Compression is where labels fall off.

**Because.** Summary layers detach real numbers from what they measured. In one research session here, adversarial verification of eight anchor claims found **four** carrying accurate digits with wrong labels: two metrics of one model read as two models; a share of coded interview fragments presented as a share of people (*by a peer-reviewed review misreading its own source*); an abstract's headline figure untraceable to any results table; a stale figure quoted as current state. None fabricated — all wrong. The same failure was independently observed, twice, in the owner's prior projects when machine steps edited judgment content.

**Failure signs.** A percentage with no visible denominator. The same figure everywhere, with every citation pointing at another citation. Components that don't sum (accuracy + error ≠ 100% usually means a third outcome is hiding). A ranking that flips depending on which column you read.

**Boundaries.** Order-of-magnitude context for low-stakes conversation doesn't need the ceremony. The trigger is: the number will inform a decision, a publication, or a disagreement.

**Provenance.** Research — session R1 (see [journal](../journal/2026-08-13-r1-lessons.md), decision D-009/D-010); converging `[anecdote]` evidence from prior projects (D-011: pattern level).
