# Charter

## Mission

Build the guide we wish existed when we started: an efficient learning path into using AI well, for smart people without a CS background. Capture hard-won lessons in durable form, so a newcomer can learn in days what took months to figure out — and so our own practice keeps improving as the field moves.

## Audience

Someone who:

- is intelligent and self-directed, but new to AI beyond casual chat use;
- doesn't program for a living (may script a little, or not at all);
- wants working mental models and judgment, not just recipes;
- has real work to do, a budget, and things to protect: data, money, reputation, and their own thinking.

We assume no CS degree, no ML background, and no patience for mystification in either direction — hype or doom.

## Non-goals

- **Not product documentation.** Vendor docs are authoritative for their products; we link, we don't mirror.
- **Not news.** We digest developments into durable lessons; we don't race to cover them.
- **Not an academic survey.** We cite primary sources but optimize for usable judgment.
- **Not a prompt-pack.** Techniques appear only with when/why/failure-modes attached.

## Editorial principles

1. **Durable over trendy.** Separate what survives model generations from what expires; label everything with its expected half-life honestly.
2. **Evidence-graded.** Load-bearing claims carry labels (defined in [method/research-protocol.md](method/research-protocol.md)): established / corroborated / single-source / contested / anecdote. Our own experience is welcome evidence — labeled as anecdote.
3. **Practice-first.** A concept earns its place by changing a decision the reader makes. Every chapter answers "so what do I do differently?"
4. **Honest about unknowns.** "Nobody knows yet" is a valid, useful sentence. Contested questions stay visibly contested.
5. **Self-demonstrating.** The repo is built with AI under its own rules — gated budgets, verification before trust, decision logs, hygiene scans. When a practice fails us here, it gets revised here.
6. **Dated where perishable.** Snapshot pages open with "As of &lt;month year&gt;" and a review-by date; stale pages get refreshed or demoted, never silently trusted.
7. **Plain language.** Jargon only when it pays rent, and defined on first use. Respect the reader: smart is not the same as informed.

## Durability classes

| Class | Meaning | Examples | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `durable` | Survives model generations | how LLMs work, verification habits | yearly sanity check |
| `semi` | Framing stable, details drift | prompting techniques, efficiency tactics | ~6 months |
| `dated` | Snapshot; decays quickly | model landscape, tool comparisons | ~3 months or on major releases |

## Success criteria

- A newcomer can go from zero to competent, safe, budget-sane AI use by reading Parts I–III of the [syllabus](SYLLABUS.md) and applying the practice cards.
- Each chapter's claims trace to logged research or labeled experience.
- The owner's own AI use measurably improves: fewer avoidable failures, better spend, faster orientation on new developments.
