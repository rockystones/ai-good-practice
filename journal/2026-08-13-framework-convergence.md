# 2026-08-13 — Convergence: two methods, one shape

**Context.** At the owner's direction, reviewed the reusable framework they extracted from two prior multi-agent reconstruction projects (domains withheld per D-011), to see what transfers into this repo's method.

**The observation worth keeping.** That framework and this repo's research protocol were developed on different project types — structured corpus reconstruction there, prose research synthesis here — and independently converged on the same core moves: pre-register the question and its falsifiers before searching; verify adversarially (refute-framed, not confirmation-seeking); treat hostile review as *input to verification*, never as authority; tier evidence and cap confidence at what the best source supports; stop on dry rounds, not quotas. Independent convergence from different project shapes is exactly the kind of evidence this guide exists to collect: these look like **durable practices of working with AI**, not artifacts of one project.

**Adopted into the method (D-010).** Checkpoint-rule file output for fan-out agents; pre-registered numeric bands; per-session telemetry; base-error spot audits; angle sole-contribution accounting; spot-checks on perfect-looking results; an owner-review protocol with blind-first and adjudicate-don't-auto-apply; a post-draft number diff.

**Left behind, deliberately.** The corpus machinery (schema/vault/build pipeline, lens packs, graph metrics, significance anchoring, federation) solves problems a prose guide doesn't have. Reuse judgment cuts both ways: importing structure you don't need is how methods calcify.

**One replicated finding now governs our own review stage.** Across both of the owner's builds, hostile reviewers were right on most checkable facts and wrong on most structural judgments. Verify the facts; weigh the structure; auto-apply neither — including when the reviewer is the owner.
